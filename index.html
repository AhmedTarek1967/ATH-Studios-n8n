How it works

  1. Applying to Job: a form takes the application and CV, checks it, and stores it.
  2. Cv Review: AI reads a CV against a job post and emails a coaching report.
  3. Rag + Policy Loader: policy documents are loaded into a knowledge base; the chat answers only from them.
  4. Ai Action: AI-powered processing of the recruitment flow.
  5. Error Handeling: failures are logged and reported so nothing breaks silently.
